Privacy Policy

How SplatCap 3D handles inquiry, project, and service information.

Information we collect

We collect information you submit through contact forms or direct email, such as your name, email address, project details, site information, and scheduling requirements.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, prepare estimates, plan capture work, deliver requested services, and support completed projects.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers used to operate this website, host viewers, process forms, or deliver the service, only as needed for those purposes.

Project data

Project files and scan data are retained only as long as needed to deliver and support the project unless a different retention period is agreed in writing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@splatcap3d.com.
