Choose the Right Way to Show a Space

Photos, video, virtual tours, and technical scans solve different problems. SplatCap 3D is designed for situations where people need to explore a real environment and understand its layout remotely.

Concise Comparison

FormatBest forLimitation
PhotographyPolished highlights and listings.Does not explain circulation or full layout.
VideoGuided storytelling and promotion.The viewer cannot choose where to look.
Traditional virtual tourBasic room-to-room viewing.Can feel segmented or less natural in complex spaces.
Technical scan dataSurvey, engineering, BIM, and measurement.Often too technical or heavy for public-facing use.
SplatCap 3DPhotorealistic, self-directed spatial exploration.Not a replacement for survey-grade data where certified accuracy is required.

Decision Guide

Choose photography when

You need a small set of polished marketing images.

Choose video when

You need a controlled story with pacing, narration, or music.

Choose technical LiDAR or photogrammetry when

You need measured, georeferenced, engineering, or CAD deliverables.

Choose SplatCap 3D when

The viewer needs to explore the space independently and understand layout, scale, and atmosphere.
