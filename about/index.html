Reality-Capture Experience Behind Every Tour

SplatCap 3D is a service of Drone Services Canada Inc., a Hamilton-based reality-capture company serving clients across Ontario.

Field Experience, Clear Delivery

Our team has more than eight years of experience capturing real sites with drones, LiDAR, photogrammetry, thermal cameras, and specialized inspection systems. SplatCap 3D applies that field experience to fast, photorealistic 3D tours designed for straightforward browser viewing.

Planned capture

We assess access, lighting, site activity, and the intended audience before capture begins.

Efficient field work

Walk-through scanning reduces disruption compared with slow stop-and-shoot workflows.

Clear delivery

Clients receive a hosted experience that can be opened by link or embedded on a website.

Technical options when required

Assignments that need survey control, CAD, LiDAR, or photogrammetry can be supported through Drone Services Canada Inc.

Service Area

Based in Hamilton, we serve Toronto, the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area, Niagara, Waterloo Region, and projects throughout Ontario.
