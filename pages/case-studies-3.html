What a Project Usually Includes

Core Delivery
A shareable spatial experience.
  • Capture planning around the site, audience, access, and end use.
  • On-site walk-through capture with coverage checks before departure.
  • Photoreal processing into a browser-accessible 3D model.
  • Hosted viewer link ready for clients, teams, or the public.
Optional Enhancements
A more guided or branded final result.
  • Website embeds, guided views, annotations, screenshots, or teaser video exports.
  • Private access control for internal or stakeholder-only viewing.
  • Recurring captures for construction progress or operational updates.
  • White-label presentation for premium client-facing experiences.
