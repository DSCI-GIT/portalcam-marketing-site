Why It Looks Different

SplatCap 3D
A more photographic, more believable spatial experience.
  • Walk-through capture creates a natural sense of movement through a real space.
  • Gaussian splat rendering preserves visual richness, depth, and atmosphere.
  • The final result feels closer to being there, not just viewing a simplified model.
  • Presentation quality supports marketing, stakeholder review, training, and premium client-facing work.
Practical Documentation
Useful visual records without making the workflow difficult.
  • Capture can be planned around active sites, complex layouts, and limited access windows.
  • Browser-accessible viewers make the scan easy to share with clients, teams, and decision-makers.
  • Hosted links and embeds reduce file handoff issues and keep the experience simple to open.
  • Optional access control and branded presentation can support client-facing delivery.
