The Process

1

Scope

We confirm the space, approximate size, access constraints, safety requirements, and how the final scan will be used.

2

Capture

Walk-through capture records the environment efficiently while keeping movement natural and disruption low.

3

Process

The capture is reconstructed, optimized, and prepared for clean browser viewing.

4

Deliver

You receive a hosted viewer, shareable link, embed option, and supporting delivery details based on the project scope.
