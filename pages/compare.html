Photoreal 3D Tours Built for Real Spatial Understanding

SplatCap 3D sits between fast marketing media and heavy technical reality-capture workflows: more immersive than photos or video, more photographic than conventional virtual tours, and easier to share than raw scan data.

SplatCap 3D vs Common Alternatives

Option Best For Where It Falls Short SplatCap 3D Advantage
Still Photography Fast highlights, listing galleries, social posts. Limited layout context and no natural movement through the space. Viewers can explore circulation, scale, and atmosphere from one hosted experience.
Video Walkthroughs Guided storytelling and short promotional edits. The viewer cannot choose their own path or pause in the exact area they care about. Interactive viewing lets buyers, guests, teams, and stakeholders inspect the space at their own pace.
Traditional Virtual Tours Basic remote viewing and room-to-room navigation. Can feel staged, segmented, or less photographic in complex real environments. Gaussian splat rendering preserves richer visual detail, depth, and atmosphere.
Technical Scan Data Engineering, survey, BIM, and measurement-led workflows. Often too heavy or technical for marketing, training, and public-facing communication. Hosted browser viewing keeps the result easy to open, share, and embed.

When SplatCap 3D Is the Better Fit

People Need to Understand Layout

Real estate buyers, venue clients, construction stakeholders, and facility visitors can understand flow and scale before they arrive.

Presentation Quality Matters

Photoreal capture creates a stronger first impression than a basic scan or folder of still images.

The Site Is Hard to Revisit

A strong hosted 3D record can reduce repeated site walks, re-shoots, and fragmented photo requests.

Decision Guide

1

Use Photos
